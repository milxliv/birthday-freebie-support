# Get support for Birthday Freebie Concierge

## Report a problem

1. Go to the issue chooser:

   https://github.com/milxliv/birthday-freebie-support/issues/new/choose

2. Pick a template:
   - **Bug report**: the app crashes, freezes, shows something wrong, or a feature doesn't work.
   - **Broken offer**: an offer is wrong, expired, or not honored, or its signup link is broken.
3. Fill in the fields, and then click **Create**.

You need a free GitHub account to open an issue. If you don't have one, email the same details
to [support@ic-lancaster.com](mailto:support@ic-lancaster.com).

Lancaster Agile Systems LLC operates Birthday Freebie Concierge.

## What to include

- **Device**: for example, iPhone 15.
- **iOS version**: on your iPhone, go to **Settings > General > About > iOS Version**.
- **Offer name**: the merchant and the offer, if the problem involves one.
- **Screenshot**: crop out personal details first.
- **What happened**: what you did, what you expected, and what you saw instead.

## Keep personal details out

Issues are public. Don't post your email address, phone number, street address, birthday, or
any redemption code a merchant gave you.

## Response time

Support is best effort. Expect a reply within a few days.

## Privacy

For what the app keeps and how to delete it, read the privacy policy in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
