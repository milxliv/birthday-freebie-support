# Birthday Freebie Concierge support

Birthday Freebie Concierge is an iOS app that finds birthday loyalty rewards near you, guides you
through signing up for each program, and tracks the deadlines so you don't miss a reward. It
never asks for your merchant passwords or access to your email. Nothing is automated: you sign up
yourself, on the merchant's own website or app, and Birthday Freebie Concierge shows you what to
do and when. The app is independent and isn't affiliated with the merchants it lists.
Lancaster Agile Systems LLC operates it.

## Get help

Open an issue and pick the template that fits:

https://github.com/milxliv/birthday-freebie-support/issues/new/choose

- **Bug report**: the app crashes, freezes, shows something wrong, or a feature doesn't work.
- **Broken offer**: an offer is wrong, expired, or not honored, or its signup link is broken.

[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) lists what to include and how soon to expect an answer.

Issues are public. Don't post your email address, phone number, street address, birthday, or
any redemption code a merchant gave you.

## Report a broken offer

Offers change, and merchants don't always announce it. If an offer in the app is wrong, open a
**Broken offer** issue:

https://github.com/milxliv/birthday-freebie-support/issues/new?template=broken_offer.md

Tell us the merchant, your ZIP code, and what happened. Add a screenshot if you have one. Common
problems:

- The reward is different from what the app shows.
- The offer has ended.
- The signup link is broken or goes to the wrong page.
- A store didn't honor the offer.
